
## This one is set facing

The reader wants the original beside your translation, so every body block comes
in a pair, in this order:

```
SRC   the original sentence or paragraph, exactly as it was written
P     your translation of it
```

The `SRC` line is the author's own words, copied across untouched apart from the
repairs above — broken words rejoined, page numbers dropped. Do not translate,
paraphrase, tidy or shorten it.

Pair every `P` with the `SRC` it renders, and keep them one to one: where the
target language wants two sentences for the author's one, both sentences go in
the single `P` that faces that `SRC`. Never let the pairs drift out of step —
a reader comparing them line by line is the whole reason for this mode.

Headings pair the same way: `SRC` then `H2`. `TITLE`, `KICKER`, `DECK`,
`BYLINE`, `NOTE` and `TERM` stand alone and take no `SRC`.
